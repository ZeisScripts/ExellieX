Privacy Policy
Last updated November 2026


This page is a simple landing page and does not provide account registration, payment, or contact forms. Here is what to expect when you visit or join the community.

Website information
The site itself does not ask you to submit personal information. The hosting provider may process basic technical request data, such as your IP address, browser details, and the pages requested, to deliver and protect the site.

Discord and external links
Choosing the Discord link takes you to a third-party service. Discord handles information associated with your account and activity under its own privacy policy and settings; we do not control that processing.

Community sharing
Avoid posting passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in public channels. Information you share with the community may be visible to other members and handled under Discord’s policies.

Questions and changes
For questions about information shared in the community, contact the moderators through Discord. This policy may be updated as the site or its services change.
